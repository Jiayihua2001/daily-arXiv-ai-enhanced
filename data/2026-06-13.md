Abstract: Kinetically trapped perovskites offer a unique platform for controlling functional properties through non-equilibrium phase formation. Our findings demonstrate that kinetic control of phase selection provides a powerful strategy for tuning polar order and multifunctional properties in hybrid perovskites, with implications for optoelectronic and photonic applications.
